About Cockcluck

Overview

Cock Cluck is an AI-powered sports technology startup modernizing the global cockfighting industry. We replace traditional betting-driven competitions with sponsor-backed tournaments, AI-assisted judging, real-time animal welfare monitoring, and a global community platform. Our mission is to help farmers build sustainable income while transforming a fragmented traditional industry into a transparent, data-driven ecosystem. We currently operate a legal livestream platform with paying subscribers and are developing our AI Computer Vision MVP, positioning Cock Cluck as the first company building an integrated AI ecosystem for the cockfightingindustry. It is also ready to promote the sport of cockfighting as a form of Thai soft power that generates sustainable income for Thai farmers.

Safety First

Animal Welfare Is
Our Central Pillar.

Strict protocols ensure integrity in every competition — this is non-negotiable.

Veterinary Team In The Ring

Veterinarians monitor each animal throughout the competition, assessing condition in real time. and Medical teams have full authority to stop any match with a risk of serious injury.

Smart Assistant

Supported by an AI-driven computer vision system, the setup continuously monitors the animals' health and analyzes performance in real-time, relaying data to the veterinary and Cutman teams so they can accurately assess conditions and provide targeted treatment.

Change in risk

The goal is to identify the best performer—before unnecessary injuries occur.
Farmers invest a significant amount of time in breeding high-quality gamecocks, yet a single match can result in the loss of the value they have painstakingly built up; therefore, this method helps mitigate risks regarding the animal's safety and the potential loss of its value.
