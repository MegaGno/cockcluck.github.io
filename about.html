About Cockcluck

Elevating the Cockfighting
Thai industry into a form
of soft power.

Cock Cluck is a platform dedicated to advancing the Thai gamecock industry and boosting farmers' incomes; it leverages AI to elevate the activity from a traditional local pastime to a professional international sport that generates stable revenue for farmers.

01

A Modern Vision

Leveraging expertise in computer engineering and over two decades of experience within the cockfighting arena business sector to elevate the industry—transforming it from a traditional local pastime into a modern, professional sport.

02

Unique Knowledge

Years of experience in the Cockfighting Thai scene revealed gaps that others overlook.

03

Perfect Hybrid

Utilizing AI technology to assist on-site veterinarians in analyzing the safety and well-being of the animals, thereby effectively promoting the safety of rooster.

Sponsorship-Driven Tournament

Revolutionizing the competition with a new format.

The competition is organized in a tournament format that awards prizes and bonuses instead of traditional betting, thereby shedding the old image associated with wagering and providing grassroots farmers with an equal opportunity to create value for their rooster.

1

No Bets From Owners

Sponsors fund the competitions, eliminating direct financial risk for owners.

2

Tournament Format

The competition consists of just three rounds, utilizing AI to analyze and tally scores to determine the winner, ensuring transparency.

3

International Sport

Elevating cockfighting into a professional sport meeting international standards will attract enthusiasts from around the world, helping to build a solid foundation and generate sustainable revenue for the Thai cockfighting industry.
