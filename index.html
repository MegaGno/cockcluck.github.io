The Introduction

The Story Behind Cock Cluck

Cock Cluck is inspired by the natural call of a rooster—a sound used to communicate, protect its territory, and connect with its flock. In the same spirit, we created this platform to give the Thai cockfighting community a voice of its own: a place where breeders, farmers, enthusiasts, and professionals can communicate freely, share knowledge, and grow together

Cock Cluck is more than just a marketplace or an online platform. Our mission is to empower every Thai cockfighting farm by creating greater value and sustainable income through innovation. We aim to transform traditional cockfighting competitions into a modern, internationally recognized professional sport with higher standards, greater transparency, and global accessibility.

Our vision includes organizing large-scale tournament competitions that are broadcast worldwide. Every team will be able to participate free of charge, with no betting required. Instead, prize money will be provided by sponsors and awarded to both winners and participants. This creates equal opportunities for grassroots farmers to showcase their breeding quality, gain recognition, and build sustainable income without relying on gambling.

Technology is at the heart of our future. We plan to integrate AI Computer Vision into competitions for real-time analysis, including animal health assessment, transparent scoring assistance, and the development of a comprehensive cockfighting bloodline database to support long-term breeding improvement and research.

Having grown up in the cockfighting industry, our passion goes far beyond building another platform. We founded Cock Cluck with the vision of transforming the entire industry into one that is transparent, standardized, technology-driven, and sustainable. As artificial intelligence continues to reshape every industry, we believe the Thai cockfighting community deserves the opportunity to evolve alongside it while preserving its rich cultural heritage for future generations.

— Natthakorn Sinamnuaiphon Founder, Cock Cluck

How come KU rooster

KU Rooster was inspired by the rich history of Kasetsart University, where the rooster has long been recognized as one of the university's symbolic animals and represents its enduring contribution to Thailand's poultry industry. One of the university's founders, Luang Suwanvajokkasikit, is widely honored as the "Father of Poultry Farming in Thailand." For decades, Kasetsart University has played a leading role in poultry research, breeding development, and knowledge transfer, helping improve the livelihoods of farmers across the country.

As a Computer Engineering student at Kasetsart University and someone who grew up in a family that has been involved in the Thai cockfighting industry for more than 20 years, Nattakorn Sinamnuayphon recognized a significant gap. While commercial poultry farming has continuously benefited from technological advancement and research, rooster farmers have received far less support in terms of technology, data, and innovation.

Driven by this vision, a group of Computer Engineering students from the Faculty of Engineering at Kasetsart University came together to establish KU Rooster. Our mission is to apply Artificial Intelligence (AI), Computer Vision, and Data Technology to modernize the cockfighting industry by promoting higher standards of Animal Welfare, reducing risks for farmers, creating sustainable economic opportunities, and positioning Thailand's cockfighting industry on the global stage as a form of Thai Soft Power.

Today, KU Rooster is committed to bridging technology and agriculture, combining engineering, artificial intelligence, and local expertise to build a more transparent, sustainable, and innovation-driven future for the Thai cockfighting community.

The history

The History & Evolution of Thai Cockfighting

Thai cockfighting is a traditional sport and cultural heritage that has been deeply rooted in Thai society for over a thousand years. It originated when ancient people domesticated wild junglefowls for food and noticed their natural fighting instincts, leading to selective breeding for entertainment and sport. This combat sport is so deeply embedded in Thai history and literature that it features in a legendary historical event during the Ayutthaya period. King Naresuan the Great famously used his "Yellow White-Tailed" rooster to defeat the Burmese Crown Prince's fighting cock, proving that cockfighting in Thailand is viewed not just as gambling, but as an honorable martial art of animals.

Today, Thai cockfighting has evolved from a rural pastime into a high-value sports industry and agricultural business. Premium breeds like the Burmese, Pakoiy, and purebred Thai cockfighting are continually developed for enhanced tactical skills and endurance. This transformation has turned cockfightingThai into a highly popular traditional sports content on online platforms and streaming services, successfully preserving this cultural legacy and conneting it with a new generation of global audiences.

The Legend Story

Chao Tham Thong

The story of "Chao Tham Thong," a grassroots farmer's fighting rooster bought cheaply by a wealthy investor, won 33 million baht in major arenas and was resold for breeding at 22 million baht, highlights systemic inequality. Due to a lack of capital to access standard arenas, the farmer missed out on becoming a millionaire. However, a fair, risk-free competition platform with proper animal welfare would empower all farmers to unlock the true value of their roosters, ensuring equal opportunities and preventing exploitation.
