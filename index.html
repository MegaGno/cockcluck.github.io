The Problem

Structural Barriers
Hold The Sport Back.

The current market faces obstacles that prevent growth and professionalization.

High Investment Risk

Owners take on significant financial risk without protection or a guaranteed return structure.

Lack of Standardization

The absence of uniform rules and animal-welfare protocols undermines credibility.

Weak Engagement

Events lack narrative, rivalries, and storytelling that create emotional connection with the audience.

Restricted Digital Channels

Without a dedicated platform, the sport remains invisible to global audiences and sponsors.

The Solution

Four Pillars.
One Professional Sport.

01

Sponsored Tournaments

A sponsorship-funded model that eliminates direct betting by owners.

02

Entertainment Format

Rivalries and narratives that build emotional engagement with fans.

03

Animal Welfare

Specialized veterinary standards and strict safety protocols at every event.

04

Vertical Platform

A dedicated digital ecosystem for community, streaming, and competitions.
